Privacy Policy

What We Store Locally

Vahntra stores saved fits and a small amount of session state directly in your browser using localStorage and sessionStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to Vahntra's servers.

Clearing your browser's site data will permanently remove your saved fits.

Cookies and Analytics

Vahntra does not currently run analytics or tracking cookies. If an analytics provider is connected in the future, this policy will be updated to disclose exactly what is collected and why.

Newsletter and Contact Forms

The newsletter signup and contact form on this site are not yet connected to a live email provider or backend. No submissions are stored or transmitted until a provider is configured, and this page will be updated when that changes.

Third-Party Links

Vahntra links to Amazon for every purchase. Amazon's own privacy policy governs any information you provide during checkout on Amazon.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our Contact page.
